# Arcade audio candidates (CC0 / public domain)

Generated 2026-10-25 from Openverse (Freesound CC0 and public-domain items), the keyless library already connected to Visionex. **Nothing here is shipped or wired.** Each row is a *candidate*: it was found by keyword and checked automatically, never auditioned by a person. Open the landing page, listen, and approve the ones you want; approved clips are processed (trim, fade, 48 kHz stereo, normalise), recorded in `audioLibrary.ts` with their licence and wired into the games, as done for the five clips in #502.

## How candidates were checked

- Licence filter: CC0 and public-domain mark only, so no attribution is owed.
- Length: effects 0.12-6 s, ambience up to 15 s, per event.
- Automatic checks on each preview: sample rate (44.1 kHz or more), peak level (clipping or too quiet), share of near-silence. These catch bad files, not wrong or ugly sounds.
- Source quality: Freesound previews are 128 kbps MP3; a better master needs a Freesound account.
- The first candidate of each event is the best-scoring one by those checks, not necessarily the best sound.
